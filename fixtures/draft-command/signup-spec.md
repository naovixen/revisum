# Sign-up

## Background

The sign-up page lives at /signup. Internal design note, never for the planner: BACKGROUND-ONLY-NOTE-7Q4.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: When a visitor submits a valid email address, the page shall show the heading "Welcome".
- AC-2: When the email address is already registered, the page shall say "That address is taken".
- AC-3: When the email field is empty, the "Create account" button shall stay disabled.
